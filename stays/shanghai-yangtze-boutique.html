1930s Shanghai Glamour Authentically Restored

The Yangtze Boutique Shanghai occupies a meticulously restored 1930s Art Deco building that once housed the legendary Yangtze Insurance Company during Shanghai's golden age. This boutique heritage hotel captures the sophisticated glamour of pre-war Shanghai, when the city was known as the "Paris of the East" and served as Asia's most cosmopolitan destination.

Every detail of the restoration honors the building's original 1930s character while incorporating modern luxury amenities. From the hand-painted ceiling murals to the restored terrazzo floors, original brass fixtures, and period furniture, guests are transported to the era when Shanghai was the playground of international socialites, artists, and adventurers.

Heritage Rooms with Period Character

Each of the 62 guest rooms and suites features carefully preserved architectural details from the 1930s, including original hardwood floors, high ceilings with decorative moldings, and restored windows that flood the spaces with natural light. The rooms blend period authenticity with contemporary comfort, featuring antique furnishings, vintage textiles, and bathrooms that combine original fixtures with modern amenities.

The Yangtze Suite, occupying the building's former executive offices, showcases the most impressive period details including hand-painted wall murals, original chandeliers, and a private balcony overlooking historic Nanjing Road. This extraordinary accommodation provides guests with an authentic experience of how Shanghai's elite lived during the city's most glamorous era.

Authentic 1930s Dining & Entertainment

The hotel's restaurant occupies the building's original dining room, serving a menu that blends traditional Shanghai cuisine with international dishes popular during the 1930s era. The kitchen sources ingredients from local markets and traditional suppliers, maintaining culinary authenticity while meeting contemporary standards.

The Jazz Age Bar recreates the sophisticated cocktail culture of 1930s Shanghai, featuring period-appropriate recipes, vintage spirits, and live jazz performances on weekends. The bar's atmosphere, with its original fixtures and period furniture, provides an authentic setting for evening entertainment that honors Shanghai's legendary nightlife heritage.

Heritage Location & Cultural Access

  • Historic Nanjing Road: Located on Shanghai's most famous heritage shopping street
  • Art Deco Walking Distance: Multiple 1930s buildings within easy walking distance
  • The Bund: 10-minute walk to Shanghai's iconic waterfront and historic architecture
  • Yu Garden: 15-minute walk to traditional Chinese gardens and old city
  • Shanghai Museum: 20-minute walk to world-class cultural institution
  • Metro Access: People's Square Station (5 minutes) for citywide connections

Heritage Tours & Cultural Experiences

The Yangtze's concierge team specializes in Shanghai heritage experiences, offering private Art Deco walking tours, visits to traditional craftspeople, and access to preserved historic buildings not typically open to the public. The hotel partners with local historians and cultural institutions to provide guests with authentic insights into Shanghai's golden age and its lasting influence on the modern city.
