Lost in Translation Fame

Tokyo's Cinematic Luxury Legend

Park Hyatt Tokyo has achieved legendary status as one of the world's most recognizable luxury hotels, thanks in part to its starring role in Sofia Coppola's "Lost in Translation." Occupying floors 39 through 52 of the Shinjuku Park Tower, this architectural masterpiece offers guests an unparalleled luxury experience suspended high above one of the world's most dynamic cities.

The hotel's design philosophy centers on creating an oasis of tranquility amidst Tokyo's kinetic energy. With its minimalist aesthetic, natural materials, and floor-to-ceiling windows offering panoramic views of Mount Fuji, Tokyo Bay, and the sprawling urban landscape, Park Hyatt Tokyo perfectly embodies the sophisticated restraint of Japanese luxury hospitality.

Elevated Accommodations with Tokyo Views

Each of the 177 guest rooms and suites features expansive windows showcasing Tokyo's magnificent skyline, with many offering glimpses of Mount Fuji on clear days. The rooms blend contemporary comfort with Japanese design sensibilities, featuring custom furnishings, marble bathrooms with deep soaking tubs, and technology seamlessly integrated into the minimalist aesthetic.

The Park Suite and Presidential Suite provide the ultimate Tokyo accommodation experience, with wraparound views, separate living and dining areas, and marble bathrooms featuring rainfall showers and panoramic city vistas. The attention to detail reflects the highest standards of Japanese hospitality, creating spaces that feel both luxurious and serene.

Iconic Dining Above Tokyo

The New York Grill, located on the 52nd floor, offers one of Tokyo's most spectacular dining experiences with panoramic city views and a menu featuring premium steaks and fresh seafood. The restaurant's wine cellar contains over 1,600 labels, one of the most extensive collections in Asia. The adjacent New York Bar provides an intimate setting for evening cocktails with live jazz performances and those unforgettable views featured in "Lost in Translation."

The Peak Lounge on the 41st floor serves refined afternoon tea and cocktails in an atmosphere of understated elegance. The space features comfortable seating areas with panoramic windows, making it perfect for both business meetings and leisurely conversations while watching Tokyo's urban rhythm unfold below.

Elevated Wellness & Recreation

Club on the Park spa provides a tranquil retreat with treatments that blend international techniques with traditional Japanese wellness practices. The facility includes private treatment rooms with city views, relaxation areas, and a comprehensive menu of massages, facials, and body treatments using premium products.

The swimming pool on the 47th floor offers a unique aquatic experience with floor-to-ceiling windows providing panoramic Tokyo views. The adjacent fitness center features state-of-the-art equipment and personal training services, all while suspended high above one of the world's most vibrant cities.

Prime Shinjuku Location & Access

  • Shinjuku Station: 10-minute walk to one of the world's busiest transportation hubs
  • Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building: Adjacent to the hotel for official business
  • Shibuya District: 15-minute train ride to iconic crossing and shopping
  • Traditional Tokyo: Easy access to Meiji Shrine, Imperial Palace, and historic districts
  • Narita Airport: 60-90 minutes via Narita Express or hotel car service
  • Haneda Airport: 45-60 minutes via monorail and train connections
