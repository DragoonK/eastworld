Where Bold Design Meets Osaka Energy

W Osaka bursts onto the scene as the city's most distinctive luxury hotel, bringing Marriott's signature W brand aesthetic to Japan's culinary capital. Since opening in 2021, this 27-story tower has become a magnet for design enthusiasts, creative professionals, and travelers seeking an experience that perfectly captures Osaka's vibrant, unconventional spirit.

The hotel's design philosophy revolves around "Whatever/Whenever" service and bold architectural statements that challenge traditional luxury hospitality norms. From the dramatic lobby featuring local art installations to the Instagram-worthy WOOBAR with panoramic city views, every space is crafted to stimulate, inspire, and surprise.

Luxury Redefined Through Vibrant Innovation

W Osaka's 337 rooms and suites showcase bold color palettes, cutting-edge technology, and playful design elements that reflect both international sophistication and local Osakan creativity. The hotel's standout feature is the rooftop infinity pool offering 360-degree views of the city, creating an urban oasis that feels worlds away from the bustling streets below.

The property houses several distinctive dining and entertainment venues, including the signature WOOBAR, which has quickly become Osaka's most buzzworthy rooftop destination. The hotel's spa combines traditional Japanese wellness practices with modern treatments, while the state-of-the-art fitness center caters to health-conscious travelers who refuse to compromise their routines.

"W Osaka isn't just accommodation—it's a lifestyle destination that amplifies the energy, creativity, and unstoppable spirit of modern Japan. We're here to fuel passion and inspire whatever's next." - Hotel Management
