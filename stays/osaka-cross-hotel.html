Contemporary Design Meets Osaka Energy

Cross Hotel Osaka epitomizes the vibrant energy of modern Osaka with its sleek, contemporary design and prime location in the heart of Dotonbori. Opened in 2019, this design-forward hotel has quickly established itself as a favorite among travelers seeking style, comfort, and authenticity in Japan's kitchen.

The hotel's architecture reflects Osaka's dynamic spirit through its bold geometric forms and striking use of natural materials. Each of the 285 rooms features floor-to-ceiling windows offering panoramic views of the bustling city below, while the interior design seamlessly blends Japanese aesthetics with international sophistication.

In the Heart of Dotonbori

The hotel's location is unbeatable for experiencing authentic Osaka culture. Situated directly above Shinsaibashi Station, guests have immediate access to the city's extensive subway network while being steps away from the iconic Dotonbori district with its neon-lit entertainment quarter, street food vendors, and historic landmarks.

The property features multiple dining options, including a signature restaurant showcasing modern Japanese cuisine and a rooftop bar offering stunning city views. The hotel's spa and fitness facilities provide a peaceful retreat from the bustling streets below, while the concierge team excels at helping guests discover both popular attractions and hidden local gems.

"We designed Cross Hotel Osaka to be more than just accommodation—it's a gateway to experiencing the authentic energy and culture of our incredible city." - Hotel Management

  • Cross Superior ($160-200/night) - Spacious 28m² rooms on higher floors with enhanced city panoramas

  • Cross Suite ($280-350/night) - Luxurious 45m² suites with separate living area and exclusive amenities

Tips for Guests

  • Book rooms on higher floors for better views and reduced street noise
  • Take advantage of the direct station connection during busy periods
  • Visit the rooftop bar during sunset for stunning city views
  • Ask concierge for local restaurant recommendations beyond typical tourist spots
  • The hotel provides excellent maps and area guides in multiple languages
  • Early morning or late evening are best times to explore Dotonbori with fewer crowds
  • Consider the breakfast buffet—it offers both Western and Japanese options
