The Pinnacle of Tokyo Luxury

The Ritz-Carlton Tokyo represents the absolute pinnacle of luxury hospitality in Japan's capital, occupying floors 45 through 53 of the prestigious Tokyo Midtown complex in Roppongi. This ultra-luxury hotel sets the global standard for opulent accommodations, impeccable service, and attention to detail that has made The Ritz-Carlton name synonymous with the world's finest hospitality experiences.

Every aspect of the hotel reflects meticulous attention to luxury, from the handcrafted furnishings and premium materials to the legendary service that anticipates guests' needs before they're even expressed. The hotel's elevated location provides stunning views of Tokyo's skyline, Mount Fuji, and the Imperial Palace gardens, creating an atmosphere of serene grandeur high above the bustling metropolis.

Ultra-Luxury Accommodations

Each of the 247 guest rooms and suites showcases the finest materials and craftsmanship, featuring Italian marble bathrooms, handwoven silk furnishings, and floor-to-ceiling windows with breathtaking city views. The rooms blend contemporary luxury with subtle Japanese design elements, creating spaces that feel both opulent and harmonious.

The Club Level accommodations provide access to The Club Lounge, offering personalized concierge services, private check-in, and exclusive culinary presentations throughout the day. The Presidential Suite, spanning an entire floor, offers unparalleled luxury with wraparound terraces, panoramic views, and a dedicated butler service that exemplifies The Ritz-Carlton's legendary hospitality standards.

World-Class Dining & Entertainment

Azure 45, located on the 45th floor, offers contemporary French cuisine with breathtaking panoramic views of Tokyo's skyline and Mount Fuji. The restaurant's intimate atmosphere and world-class cuisine make it one of Tokyo's most sought-after dining destinations. The wine program features an exceptional collection of vintage wines and champagnes, carefully curated to complement the sophisticated menu.

The Bar provides an intimate setting for premium spirits and creative cocktails, while The Lobby Lounge serves traditional afternoon tea and light meals in an atmosphere of refined elegance. The Club Lounge offers exclusive dining experiences throughout the day for Club Level guests, featuring artisanal canapés, premium beverages, and personalized service.

Ultra-Luxury Wellness & Recreation

The Ritz-Carlton Spa provides the ultimate wellness experience with treatments that blend international techniques with traditional Japanese healing practices. The facility features private treatment suites, a relaxation lounge, and a comprehensive menu of massages, facials, and body treatments using the finest products and techniques.

The indoor swimming pool on the 46th floor offers a unique aquatic experience with floor-to-ceiling windows providing spectacular city views. The adjacent fitness center features state-of-the-art equipment, personal training services, and yoga and meditation classes, all while suspended high above Tokyo's urban landscape.
