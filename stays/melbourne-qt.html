Playful Luxury in Melbourne's Creative Heart

QT Melbourne stands boldly in the heart of the city's fashion and theatre district, offering an unapologetically playful approach to luxury hospitality. This boutique design hotel challenges conventional luxury with its eclectic aesthetic, curated art collection, and irreverent personality that perfectly captures Melbourne's creative spirit and love for the unexpected.

Located on Russell Street, QT Melbourne puts guests at the epicenter of Melbourne's cultural energy. The hotel's striking design, featuring bold patterns, unexpected color palettes, and carefully curated artworks, creates an atmosphere that's both sophisticated and whimsical - a perfect reflection of Melbourne's reputation as Australia's most culturally diverse and creative city.

Designer Rooms with Personality

Each of the 188 guest rooms and suites showcases QT's signature design philosophy through bold patterns, rich textures, and unexpected artistic touches. No two rooms are identical, with each featuring unique artwork, custom furnishings, and thoughtful details that tell a story. The rooms blend comfort with creativity, offering modern amenities within spaces that feel more like art installations than traditional hotel accommodations.

The Terrace Suites feature private outdoor spaces with city views, while the Penthouse Suite spans two levels with a private rooftop terrace. All rooms include custom QT amenities, locally-sourced minibar selections, and technology seamlessly integrated into the artistic design aesthetic.

Creative Dining & Rooftop Experience

Pascale Bar & Grill reimagines the traditional hotel restaurant concept through a lens of creativity and playfulness. The menu showcases modern Australian cuisine with unexpected twists, while the interior design creates an atmosphere that's part restaurant, part art installation. The space regularly hosts culinary events, wine tastings, and collaborative dinners with local chefs.

The Rooftop at QT provides one of Melbourne's most distinctive elevated experiences, featuring panoramic city views, creative cocktails, and a design aesthetic that changes with the seasons. The space hosts regular events, from fashion shows to art exhibitions, making it a dynamic venue that reflects Melbourne's ever-evolving cultural landscape.
