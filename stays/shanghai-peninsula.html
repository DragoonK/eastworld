Art Deco Masterpiece on the Historic Bund

The Peninsula Shanghai stands as one of the most prestigious addresses on the historic Bund, offering guests an unparalleled luxury experience in the heart of Shanghai's financial and cultural district. This magnificent Art Deco-inspired hotel seamlessly blends the glamour of 1930s Shanghai with contemporary sophistication, creating an atmosphere of timeless elegance.

Located at 32 The Bund, the hotel occupies a prime waterfront position with sweeping views of the Huangpu River and the futuristic skyline of Pudong. The building's distinctive architecture pays homage to Shanghai's golden age while incorporating cutting-edge design elements that reflect the city's dynamic present and future.

Luxury Accommodations with Bund Views

Each of the 235 guest rooms and suites features elegant Art Deco-inspired interiors with modern amenities. Floor-to-ceiling windows frame spectacular views of either the Huangpu River and Pudong skyline or the historic architecture of the Bund. Rooms feature rich fabrics, custom furniture, and marble bathrooms with deep soaking tubs and walk-in rain showers.

The hotel's signature suites, including the Peninsula Suite, offer expansive living spaces with panoramic river views, separate dining areas, and butler service. Advanced in-room technology includes bedside and bathroom control panels, complimentary WiFi, and 42-inch LCD televisions.

Exceptional Dining & Entertainment

The Peninsula Shanghai offers three distinct dining venues, each providing a unique culinary experience. Sir Elly's Restaurant, located on the 13th floor, serves contemporary European cuisine with floor-to-ceiling windows offering panoramic views of the Bund and Pudong. The adjacent Sir Elly's Terrace provides an al fresco dining option with one of the city's most romantic settings.

Yi Long Court specializes in authentic Cantonese cuisine and traditional dim sum, featuring live seafood tanks and private dining rooms for special occasions. The Compass Bar, situated in the hotel's lobby, offers an elegant setting for afternoon tea, evening cocktails, and light meals throughout the day.

World-Class Spa & Wellness

The ESPA at The Peninsula Shanghai spans an entire floor and provides a tranquil retreat from the bustling city. The spa features eight treatment rooms, including couples' suites, offering a comprehensive menu of massages, facials, and body treatments using ESPA's renowned product lines. The fitness center includes state-of-the-art equipment and personal training services.
