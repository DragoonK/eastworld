Luxury Sanctuary in East Melbourne

Park Hyatt Melbourne stands as an oasis of sophisticated luxury in the heart of Australia's cultural capital. Located in the prestigious East Melbourne precinct, this elegant hotel combines timeless design with contemporary comfort, offering guests an unparalleled experience just steps from the city's most iconic attractions including the Melbourne Cricket Ground, Fitzroy Gardens, and the bustling laneways of the CBD.

The hotel's refined aesthetic showcases the finest Australian craftsmanship with imported Italian marble, rich timber, and carefully curated artworks. Every detail reflects the Park Hyatt philosophy of understated luxury, creating an atmosphere of sophisticated tranquility that serves as a perfect retreat from Melbourne's vibrant energy.

Elegantly Appointed Accommodations

Each of the 240 guest rooms and suites features park or city views, with interiors that showcase Australian design sensibilities through rich timber furnishings, luxurious fabrics, and carefully selected contemporary artworks. The signature Italian Carrara marble bathrooms feature deep soaking tubs, separate glass-enclosed showers, and premium Malin+Goetz amenities.

The Park Suite, spanning 120 square meters, offers panoramic views of Fitzroy Gardens and the Melbourne Cricket Ground. With separate living and dining areas, a private study, and a marble bathroom with panoramic city views, it provides the ultimate in urban luxury accommodation.

Fine Dining & Premium Beverages

radii Restaurant & Bar showcases modern Australian cuisine with an emphasis on fresh, local ingredients sourced from Victoria's renowned food and wine regions. The restaurant's contemporary design features floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking Parliament House gardens, creating an elegant atmosphere for both business dinners and special occasions.

The bar offers an extensive selection of Australian wines, featuring boutique producers from the Yarra Valley, Mornington Peninsula, and beyond, alongside classic cocktails crafted with premium local spirits. The afternoon tea service has become a Melbourne institution, featuring house-made pastries and exceptional coffee roasted specifically for the hotel.

Wellness & Recreation

The hotel's Day Spa provides a tranquil sanctuary in the heart of Melbourne, offering a comprehensive menu of treatments incorporating Australian native ingredients and therapeutic techniques. The temperature-controlled indoor swimming pool and spa provide year-round relaxation, while the state-of-the-art fitness center features Technogym equipment and personal training services.

Prime Melbourne Location

  • Melbourne Cricket Ground (MCG): 5-minute walk for sporting events and tours
  • Federation Square: 10-minute walk to cultural hub and dining precincts
  • Royal Botanic Gardens: 8-minute walk to Melbourne's green oasis
  • Collins Street Shopping: 15-minute walk to luxury boutiques and department stores
  • Melbourne Airport: 30-minute drive or hotel car service available
  • Public Transport: Jolimont Station (2 minutes) provides direct access to the city loop
