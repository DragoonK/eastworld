Italian Luxury in a Shanghai Garden Oasis

Bulgari Hotel Shanghai represents the epitome of contemporary Italian luxury, nestled within a lush 8,500-square-meter private garden along the Huangpu River. This ultra-luxury hotel brings the sophisticated elegance of Roman haute couture to Shanghai, creating an urban oasis that seamlessly blends Italian design excellence with refined Chinese hospitality traditions.

The hotel's unique setting in the Suzhou Creek Heritage Conservation Area provides guests with an extraordinary escape from Shanghai's urban intensity. Designed by renowned architects Antonio Citterio and Patricia Viel, every detail reflects Bulgari's legendary commitment to craftsmanship, from the handcrafted Italian furnishings to the carefully curated contemporary art collection throughout the property.

Exquisite Accommodations with Garden Views

Each of the 82 rooms and suites showcases Italian design mastery through rich materials including ebony wood, Sahara Noir marble, and handwoven silk fabrics. Floor-to-ceiling windows offer panoramic views of either the hotel's private gardens or the Huangpu River. The signature bathrooms feature black Zimbabwe granite and silver travertine, with deep soaking tubs and rainfall showers that create a spa-like sanctuary.

The Il Giardino Villa, an exclusive 350-square-meter penthouse, offers the ultimate luxury experience with its own private terrace overlooking both the garden and river. This extraordinary accommodation features a living room, dining area, study, master bedroom, and marble bathroom, all designed with Bulgari's signature attention to detail and Italian craftsmanship.

Exceptional Italian Dining & Amenities

Il Ristorante, under the culinary direction of Michelin-starred Chef Niko Romito, presents an extraordinary Italian dining experience with ingredients sourced directly from Italy. The restaurant's terrace overlooking the hotel's private garden creates an enchanting atmosphere for both lunch and dinner. The menu showcases contemporary Italian cuisine while respecting traditional techniques and flavors.

The Bar offers an intimate setting for cocktails and light dining, featuring an extensive selection of Italian wines, grappa, and creative cocktails inspired by both Italian and Chinese flavors. The garden terraces provide multiple outdoor dining and relaxation areas, each offering different perspectives of this unique urban oasis.

Bulgari Spa & Wellness Excellence

The Bulgari Spa provides an unparalleled wellness experience using the brand's exclusive skincare and fragrance lines. The spa features private treatment rooms with garden views, a traditional hammam, and specialized treatments that blend Italian wellness traditions with ancient Chinese healing practices. The 25-meter indoor swimming pool is surrounded by floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking the garden.

Exclusive Garden Location & Access

  • Hongqiao Airport: 30-40 minutes via luxury hotel transport
