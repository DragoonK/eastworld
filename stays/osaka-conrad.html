Luxury in the Clouds Above Osaka

Conrad Osaka soars above the city skyline, occupying floors 33-40 of the Nakanoshima Festival Tower, offering guests an unparalleled bird's-eye view of Japan's vibrant culinary capital. Since opening in 2017, this Hilton luxury brand property has redefined sophisticated hospitality in Osaka, combining cutting-edge design with impeccable service standards.

Every aspect of the hotel has been meticulously crafted to maximize the stunning panoramic views. From the moment you step into the sky lobby on the 33rd floor, floor-to-ceiling windows frame the urban landscape, creating an immediate sense of grandeur and escape from the bustling streets below.

Elevated Hospitality Experience

Conrad Osaka's 164 rooms and suites are among the largest in the city, each featuring a sophisticated palette of natural materials and contemporary Japanese design elements. The hotel's signature feature is undoubtedly the views—whether overlooking Osaka Bay, the historic Osaka Castle, or the sprawling urban landscape, every room offers a unique perspective on the city.

The property features three distinctive restaurants, including the acclaimed 40 Sky Bar & Lounge on the top floor, offering creative cocktails and small plates with 360-degree city views. The spa and fitness facilities provide a serene retreat high above the urban energy, while the executive lounge offers an exclusive environment for business travelers and special occasions.

"At Conrad Osaka, we believe luxury should elevate not just your accommodation, but your entire perspective on the city. Every detail is designed to create unforgettable moments above the clouds." - Hotel Management
