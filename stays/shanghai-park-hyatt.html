The World's Highest Hotel Experience

Park Hyatt Shanghai occupies floors 79 through 93 of the iconic Shanghai World Financial Center, offering guests an unprecedented luxury experience literally above the clouds. As one of the world's highest hotels, this architectural marvel provides breathtaking panoramic views of Shanghai's sprawling metropolis from every guest room and public area.

The hotel's minimalist aesthetic, designed by renowned architect Kohn Pedersen Fox, creates an atmosphere of serene sophistication that perfectly complements the dramatic cityscape views. Clean lines, natural materials, and subtle luxury define every space, from the soaring lobby to the intimate guest rooms suspended high above the bustling streets below.

Rooms Among the Clouds

Each of the 174 guest rooms and suites features floor-to-ceiling windows offering unobstructed 270-degree views of Shanghai. The minimalist interiors showcase natural oak, granite, and silk, creating a zen-like atmosphere that encourages relaxation while providing all modern conveniences. Advanced in-room technology includes iPad controls for lighting and temperature, rainfall showers, and deep soaking tubs positioned to maximize the spectacular views.

The Chairman Suite, occupying an entire corner of the 91st floor, offers 240 square meters of pure luxury with wraparound views of the Huangpu River, the historic Bund, and Pudong's futuristic skyline. Butler service ensures every need is anticipated and fulfilled with Park Hyatt's signature attention to detail.

Sky-High Dining & Entertainment

The hotel features two exceptional dining venues that take advantage of their extraordinary heights. 100 Century Avenue, located on the 91st floor, serves contemporary cuisine with an emphasis on fresh, seasonal ingredients. The restaurant's floor-to-ceiling windows provide 360-degree views of Shanghai, creating an unparalleled dining atmosphere that changes dramatically from day to night.

The Living Room bar on the 87th floor offers an intimate setting for cocktails and light meals, featuring an extensive wine collection and innovative cocktails inspired by the hotel's sky-high location. The Observatory on the 100th floor provides access to some of the highest publicly accessible views in the world, perfect for special celebrations and photography.

Wellness in the Clouds

The hotel's spa, located on the 85th floor, offers treatments with panoramic views of Shanghai's skyline. The facility includes treatment rooms with floor-to-ceiling windows, a relaxation area, and a fitness center equipped with state-of-the-art equipment. The crown jewel is the swimming pool on the 100th floor, creating the sensation of swimming among the clouds with the city spread out far below.

Getting There & Accessibility

  • From Pudong International Airport: 30-minute taxi ride or hotel car service
  • From Hongqiao Airport: 45-minute drive during off-peak hours
  • Shanghai Maglev: Take Maglev to Longyang Road, then Metro Line 2 to Lujiazui (2-minute walk)
  • Metro Access: Lujiazui Station (Line 2) - direct connection to hotel tower
  • Location: Connected to Shanghai World Financial Center in the heart of Lujiazui financial district
