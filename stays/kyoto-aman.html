A Forest Sanctuary in Historic Kyoto

Hidden at the foot of Mount Daimonji within a once-forgotten secret garden, Aman Kyoto is a sanctuary of serene luxury that seamlessly blends with its natural surroundings. Set on 80 acres of forested grounds, this exclusive retreat offers an uncommon perspective on Japan's ancient imperial capital, merging cultural immersion with pristine natural beauty.

Designed by Kerry Hill Architects, who masterfully created other iconic Aman properties, the resort comprises a collection of modernist pavilions constructed using traditional Japanese materials and techniques. Each structure honors the cultural heritage of its setting while offering contemporary comforts and sophisticated design.

Accommodations & Experiences

The resort features 26 guest rooms and suites, housed within six pavilions that blend harmoniously with the surrounding forest. Each room is a minimalist sanctuary, featuring floor-to-ceiling windows, tatami mats, hinoki cypress ofuro bathtubs, and tokonoma alcoves showcasing local artwork.

"Aman Kyoto represents the essence of Japan's cultural and natural splendor, offering an intimate connection to place through design that honors both tradition and innovation. Here, luxury is defined by space, privacy, and immersion in the extraordinary beauty of a secret garden." - Vladimir Kojic, General Manager

Tips for Guests
