Yunnan's Exotic Flavors in Shanghai

Lost Heaven brings the exotic and diverse flavors of Yunnan province to the heart of Shanghai's historic Bund. This atmospheric restaurant offers diners a journey through the culinary traditions of China's southwestern frontier, where influences from Tibet, Myanmar, and Laos create a unique and vibrant cuisine.

The restaurant's interior reflects the mystical allure of the ancient Silk Road, with rich fabrics, antique furnishings, and dim lighting that creates an intimate, almost otherworldly dining experience. The menu showcases the incredible diversity of Yunnan cuisine, from fiery Dai dishes to delicate mushroom preparations that highlight the province's incredible biodiversity.

Signature Yunnan Specialties

Wild Mushroom Hot Pot

A celebration of Yunnan's incredible mushroom diversity, featuring rare varieties in a fragrant, nourishing broth.

Crossing the Bridge Noodles

Yunnan's most famous dish - rice noodles served in a rich, scalding broth with fresh ingredients added tableside.

Steam Pot Fish

Fresh fish cooked in Yunnan's traditional steam pot, preserving all the delicate flavors and nutrients.

The Lost Heaven Experience

What sets Lost Heaven apart is its commitment to authenticity combined with sophisticated presentation. The chefs source many ingredients directly from Yunnan province, ensuring that the flavors remain true to their origins while adapting to Shanghai's cosmopolitan palate.

The restaurant offers both à la carte dining and set menus that provide a comprehensive introduction to Yunnan cuisine. The wine list features selections that complement the bold, complex flavors of the food, including some excellent Chinese wines from Yunnan's emerging wine regions.

"Lost Heaven offers a genuine taste of Yunnan's diverse culinary landscape in an atmosphere that transports you far from Shanghai's bustling streets." - Shanghai Dining Guide
