About the Restaurant

Opened in June 2023, Yakitori Bonzo is the second location of the popular "Bonzo Honten" from Kamishinjo, which has been in business for 17 years. Located in the vibrant Shinsaibashi district, this stylish yakitori restaurant specializes in premium charcoal-grilled chicken dishes featuring fresh chicken delivered directly from Miyazaki Prefecture.

Elevated Yakitori Experience in Osaka

Yakitori Bonzo brings a refined approach to the beloved Japanese tradition of grilled chicken skewers. What sets this establishment apart is their commitment to sourcing the finest chicken directly from Miyazaki Prefecture, known for producing some of Japan's highest quality poultry. Unlike many yakitori restaurants that serve chicken on skewers, Bonzo offers a distinctive "bara-yaki" style where the meat is grilled without skewers, allowing for unique cuts and preparation methods.

The restaurant opened its Shinsaibashi location in June 2023 as the second branch of the highly regarded Bonzo Honten in Kamishinjo, which has built a loyal following over its 17 years in operation. The stylish interior creates a sophisticated yet comfortable atmosphere, making it ideal for everything from date nights to business dinners.

Charcoal-Grilled Specialties

At the heart of Yakitori Bonzo's menu is their exceptional charcoal-grilled chicken prepared over high-quality bincho charcoal. This traditional cooking method imparts a distinctive smoky flavor while sealing in the natural juices of the meat. The restaurant's signature dish is the "Charcoal-grilled Sorires," which features a particularly tender part of the chicken thigh that is carefully grilled to achieve a rare, succulent texture while maintaining food safety.

Another standout offering is their aged thigh charcoal grill, which uses a proprietary aging technique to enhance the flavor and tenderness of the meat. The chicken is brought directly from select poultry farms and prepared using methods that have been refined over the restaurant's 17-year history.

For the more adventurous diner, Yakitori Bonzo offers chicken sashimi and chicken yukke (a tartare-like preparation), showcasing the exceptional freshness and quality of their ingredients. These raw chicken preparations are possible due to the restaurant's strict sourcing standards and daily delivery of fresh chicken from Miyazaki.

"What makes our yakitori special is our commitment to using only the freshest chicken from Miyazaki, grilled over premium bincho charcoal. We don't use skewers for many of our dishes, which allows us to prepare unique cuts like our signature Sorires in ways that bring out their natural flavors and textures. Our aging process, developed over 17 years, creates a depth of flavor you won't find elsewhere in Osaka." - Chef at Yakitori Bonzo

Eastworld's Review

Food Quality: ★★★★★

Yakitori Bonzo elevates grilled chicken to an art form. Their strict adherence to using only the freshest Miyazaki chicken, cooked over premium bincho charcoal, results in exceptional flavor and texture. The unique bara-yaki (non-skewered) style allows for specialized preparation of different cuts, showcasing the restaurant's deep understanding of chicken cuisine.

Authenticity: ★★★★★

While innovating within the yakitori tradition, Bonzo maintains deep respect for the essential elements that make Japanese grilled chicken special. The restaurant offers a genuine taste of high-end Japanese yakitori that builds upon tradition rather than merely replicating it.

Atmosphere: ★★★★

The restaurant features a sophisticated design with a stylish bar counter and comfortable seating that creates an upscale yet relaxed dining environment. The open kitchen allows diners to watch the skilled preparation of dishes, adding to the experience. While elegant, it maintains a welcoming atmosphere.

Service: ★★★★

Staff are knowledgeable about the menu and can guide diners through the various specialized chicken cuts and preparations. They offer attentive service without being intrusive. While English capability varies among staff members, they make concerted efforts to accommodate international diners.

Value: ★★★½

With an average price point of ¥4,000-5,000 per person, Yakitori Bonzo sits at the premium end of the yakitori spectrum. The exceptional quality of ingredients and preparation justifies much of this cost, though it may not be suitable for budget-conscious diners. The experience and quality delivered are worth the investment for a special meal.

Overall: ★★★★½

Yakitori Bonzo offers an exceptional dining experience that showcases the depth and sophistication possible within Japanese chicken cuisine. From unique preparations like their signature Sorires to the rare offering of chicken sashimi, the restaurant provides dishes that can't be found at typical yakitori establishments. For culinary adventurers seeking to experience the heights of Japanese chicken preparation, Bonzo is a must-visit destination in Osaka.

Tips for Visitors

  • Reservations are recommended, especially for weekend evenings
  • Try the signature charcoal-grilled Sorires for a unique dish you won't find elsewhere
  • If you're feeling adventurous, the chicken sashimi is exceptionally fresh and high-quality

  • Ask the staff for sake or shochu pairing recommendations with your chicken dishes
  • The cheese tsukune is particularly popular and recommended for first-time visitors
  • Budget around ¥4,000-5,000 per person for a complete dining experience
