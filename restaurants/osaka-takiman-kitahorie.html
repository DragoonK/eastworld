70 Years of Butcher Excellence

Takiman Kitahorie represents seven decades of meat expertise, where a traditional butcher shop has evolved into one of Osaka's premier yakiniku destinations. This isn't just a restaurant—it's a testament to the art of selecting, aging, and preparing the finest Kuroge Wagyu beef.

The Takiman Legacy

What sets Takiman apart is not just the quality of the meat, but the story behind it. For 70 years, this family-run butcher shop has been perfecting the art of meat selection and preparation. The restaurant represents the culmination of this expertise, where diners can experience the difference that comes from true craftsmanship.

The atmosphere is warm and inviting, with both table seating and private rooms available. The staff's knowledge of each cut and its optimal preparation method ensures that every piece of meat is cooked to perfection, allowing the natural flavors of the premium wagyu to shine through.

Signature Specialties

Premium Kuroge Wagyu

Carefully selected A4-grade and above black wagyu beef, showcasing the perfect balance of marbling and flavor that only comes from decades of expertise.

Rare Cuts Selection

Access to exclusive cuts and rare parts that are only available through direct butcher relationships, offering a unique dining experience.

Traditional Preparation

Time-honored techniques passed down through three generations, ensuring each cut is prepared to perfection with optimal aging and seasoning.

The Dining Experience

Whether you're a yakiniku enthusiast or new to premium Japanese beef, Takiman offers an educational and delicious journey through the world of high-quality wagyu, backed by generations of expertise and passion for excellence.

The restaurant features both table seating and private rooms, making it perfect for intimate dinners, family gatherings, or corporate events. The knowledgeable staff can guide you through the menu, explaining the origin of each cut and the best cooking methods to maximize flavor.
