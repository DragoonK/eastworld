How to Get There

From Shibuya Station:

  • Exit from the Hachiko Exit
  • Walk towards the Shibuya Scramble Crossing
  • Turn right at the first street after the crossing (Dogenzaka)
  • Genki Sushi is about a 5-minute walk up the hill on your right

Innovative Sushi Experience with Tablet Ordering

Genki Sushi represents the tech-forward evolution of conveyor belt sushi restaurants in Japan. Rather than the traditional setup where sushi circulates continuously on a belt, Genki utilizes a tablet ordering system and a high-speed "Shinkansen" (bullet train) delivery system that brings your selections directly to your seat.

The restaurant's bright, modern interior with individual booths creates an efficient dining environment that's perfect for solo diners, couples, or small groups who want quality sushi at reasonable prices without the formality of traditional sushi restaurants.

Tech-Savvy Dining Experience

What sets Genki Sushi apart is its innovative ordering system. Each seat is equipped with a touchscreen tablet featuring a menu with photos of every item. Simply tap on what you want, confirm your order, and within minutes, your selections arrive on a miniature shinkansen (bullet train) that runs along tracks directly to your table.

The system not only adds an element of fun to the dining experience but also ensures that every piece of sushi is made fresh to order rather than circulating on a conveyor belt for an indeterminate amount of time.

"We wanted to combine traditional sushi with modern technology to create a new kind of dining experience—one that's efficient but doesn't sacrifice quality or freshness." - Genki Sushi Management

Menu Highlights

Genki Sushi offers an extensive menu with over 100 items, ranging from traditional nigiri to creative rolls, side dishes, and desserts. Most plates are priced between 100-500 yen, making it easy to sample a variety of options without overspending.

Some standout items include:

  • Salmon Nigiri - Consistently fresh and generously cut
  • Spicy Tuna Roll - A perfect balance of heat and flavor
  • Engawa - Flounder fin, a specialty not always found at budget sushi restaurants
  • Wagyu Beef Nigiri - Briefly seared and seasoned with a touch of wasabi
  • Tamagoyaki - Sweet Japanese omelet, a good option for those new to sushi

Eastworld's Review

Food Quality: ★★★★

While not comparable to high-end sushi establishments, Genki offers impressively fresh fish and well-prepared rice for its price point. The sushi is made to order, ensuring optimal freshness.

Value: ★★★★★

Excellent value for money in Tokyo's often expensive dining scene. Most visitors can enjoy a satisfying meal for 1000-1500 yen ($10-15).

Atmosphere: ★★★★

The modern, tech-forward environment is fun and energetic. The individual booth setup provides some privacy while maintaining the communal feel of a sushi restaurant.

Service: ★★★

The automated ordering system minimizes human interaction, but staff are available and generally helpful when needed. Water is self-serve, and you calculate your bill by stacking your plates.

Overall: ★★★★

Genki Sushi offers a perfect blend of quality, affordability, and uniquely Japanese innovation. It's an ideal spot for travelers seeking good sushi without the formality or high prices of traditional establishments.

Tips for Visitors

  • Arrive outside peak hours (12-2 PM, 6-8 PM) to avoid the longest wait times
  • Plates are color-coded by price; green plates are the most affordable options
  • The touch screen has an English option—look for the language button in the corner
  • Green tea is free and self-serve at each table
  • Payment is calculated by counting your stacked plates at the end of your meal
  • If you're dining with children, request a table rather than counter seating for more space

Visit Information

Genki Sushi does not accept reservations. For the best experience, we recommend:

  • Visiting during off-peak hours: 2-5 PM or after 9 PM
  • Being prepared for a short wait during busy periods (typically 15-30 minutes)
  • Having smaller groups (2-4 people) for faster seating

For large groups or special arrangements, you can contact the restaurant directly at +81 3-3461-1281.
