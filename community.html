Don't miss Pad Thai from the street vendors at Thip Samai, it's legendary! Also try Mango Sticky Rice at any night market, Som Tam (papaya salad), and boat noodles at Victory Monument. For areas, definitely check out Yaowarat (Chinatown) in the evening, Chatuchak Weekend Market, and Or Tor Kor Market for some of the best street food experiences.

As someone who goes to Bangkok at least twice a year, I'd suggest always looking for places with lines of locals. My personal favorites are the coconut ice cream served in a coconut shell, grilled pork skewers (Moo Ping), and crispy pancakes (Khanom Buang). For safety, make sure the food is cooked fresh in front of you, and carry hand sanitizer!
