KOBE

A sophisticated port city nestled between mountains and sea, offering a blend of international influences and traditional Japanese charm.

Kobe, Japan's sixth-largest city, is nestled between the Rokko Mountain Range and Osaka Bay. Known for its cosmopolitan atmosphere, international history, and world-famous beef, Kobe offers visitors a unique blend of Eastern and Western influences. The city rebuilt itself with remarkable resilience after the devastating Great Hanshin Earthquake of 1995, and today stands as one of Japan's most attractive and livable cities.

From historic districts like Kitano, lined with Western-style mansions from the late 19th century, to the modern harborfront at Meriken Park, Kobe showcases Japan's harmonious integration of global influences while maintaining its distinct cultural identity.

TOP ATTRACTIONS

Kobe Harborland

Harborland Open daily

A shopping and entertainment complex along the waterfront with spectacular views of Kobe Port Tower and the city skyline, especially beautiful at night.

Kitano Ijinkan District

Kitano 9:00-17:00

Historic area featuring Western-style mansions (ijinkan) built by foreign merchants in the late 19th century when Kobe was opened to foreign trade.

Kobe Port Tower

Meriken Park 9:00-21:00

The distinctive red landmark of Kobe's skyline, offering panoramic views of the city, port, and Osaka Bay from its observation decks.

Nunobiki Herb Garden

Rokko Mountain 10:00-17:00

Japan's largest herb garden with approximately 75,000 herbs and flowers, accessible via the Shin-Kobe Ropeway with stunning city views during the ascent.

Arima Onsen

North of Kobe Various hours

One of Japan's oldest hot spring resorts, famous for its gold (kinsen) and silver (ginsen) spring waters, rich in minerals with therapeutic properties.

Akashi Kaikyo Bridge

West of Kobe Bridge Exhibition: 9:00-17:00

The world's longest suspension bridge connecting Kobe to Awaji Island. Visit the Bridge Exhibition Center to learn about its construction.

GETTING AROUND

Local Transportation Tips

The Kobe Municipal Subway and JR lines connect major areas of the city. Consider the Kobe City Loop Bus for tourist attractions.

The Hankyu, Hanshin, and JR lines provide easy connections to Osaka (15-30 minutes) and Kyoto (approximately 1 hour).

The City Loop Bus is convenient for tourists, stopping at major attractions with English announcements.

The Shin-Kobe Ropeway offers a scenic way to access Nunobiki Herb Garden and enjoy panoramic city views.
