PHNOM PENH

Cambodia's resilient capital where ancient traditions and modern aspirations blend along the banks of the mighty Mekong

Phnom Penh, Cambodia's bustling capital, sits at the confluence of the Mekong and Tonlé Sap rivers. Once known as the "Pearl of Asia," this city of contrasts combines French colonial architecture with grand Khmer buildings, Buddhist temples, and modern developments.

Having emerged from a turbulent recent history, today's Phnom Penh is a vibrant, evolving metropolis where traditional markets buzz alongside hip cafés and restaurants. While the city preserves important historical sites documenting Cambodia's tragic past, it also embraces the future with dynamic arts, culture, and culinary scenes that make it one of Southeast Asia's most fascinating capitals to explore.

TOP ATTRACTIONS

Royal Palace & Silver Pagoda

Sangkat Chey Chumneah 8:00-17:00

The magnificent Royal Palace complex houses the residence of the King and the spectacular Silver Pagoda, featuring a floor made of 5,000 silver tiles and a life-sized gold Buddha adorned with 9,584 diamonds.

Tuol Sleng Genocide Museum

Tuol Sleng 8:00-17:00

A former high school converted into the infamous S-21 prison during the Khmer Rouge regime, now preserving the memory of Cambodia's tragic history with sobering exhibits and survivor testimonies.

Central Market (Phsar Thmei)

Phsar Thmei 7:00-18:00

A distinctive Art Deco building housing a bustling market where you can find everything from fresh produce and street food to jewelry, electronics, and traditional Khmer crafts.

National Museum of Cambodia

Near Royal Palace 8:00-17:00

Housed in a traditional terracotta structure, this museum displays the world's finest collection of Khmer sculptures, artifacts, and art spanning from prehistoric times to the post-Angkor period.

Wat Phnom

Wat Phnom District 7:00-18:30

The sacred temple that gave the city its name, set on a small hill with ornate stupas, Buddha statues, and beautifully painted murals. A popular spot for locals to pray for good luck.

Riverside Promenade

Sisowath Quay Always accessible

A vibrant 3km stretch along the Tonlé Sap River with parks, cafés, restaurants, and bars. Perfect for sunset strolls and observing local life, especially during evening exercise classes.

WHERE TO EAT

Romdeng Restaurant

Street 174 $$

Set in a beautiful colonial building, this social enterprise restaurant serves authentic Cambodian cuisine including Fish Amok and adventurous options like fried tarantulas, while supporting former street youth.

Foreign Correspondents' Club (FCC)

Sisowath Quay $$$

A legendary riverside institution with colonial charm, offering excellent cocktails and a menu blending Western and Asian cuisines, along with stunning Mekong views from the terrace.

Phnom Penh Night Market

Riverside $

Experience authentic Cambodian street food at this lively market where you can sit on mats while sampling grilled meats, seafood, fruit shakes, and local snacks amid a festive atmosphere.

Malis Restaurant

Norodom Blvd $$$

Led by master chef Luu Meng, this elegant restaurant serves "living Cambodian cuisine" that revives traditional recipes lost during the Khmer Rouge era, using the finest local ingredients.

WHERE TO STAY

Raffles Hotel Le Royal

Daun Penh 5-star heritage

Cambodia's most prestigious address since 1929, this historic landmark hotel offers timeless elegance with colonial charm, tropical gardens, and exceptional service in the heart of the city.

Rosewood Phnom Penh

Vattanac Capital Tower 5-star luxury

Ultra-luxury hotel occupying the top floors of Cambodia's tallest building, featuring breathtaking city views, exquisite dining options, and a stunning sky bar with an outdoor terrace.

Patio Hotel & Urban Resort

BKK1 4-star boutique

A tranquil oasis in the bustling city with a rooftop infinity pool offering panoramic views, comfortable rooms with local touches, and a great location near cafés and restaurants.

Pavilion Hotel

Near Royal Palace 4-star heritage

A collection of restored colonial villas once owned by Cambodian royalty, offering lush gardens, two swimming pools, and art deco interiors just steps from the Royal Palace.

GETTING AROUND

Local Transportation Tips

Tuk-tuks are the quintessential way to get around Phnom Penh, with fares typically ranging from $2-4 per trip. Negotiate the price before starting your journey.

Ride-hailing apps like PassApp and Grab are popular, offering air-conditioned cars and tuk-tuks with fixed fares and no need for haggling.

Several companies offer bicycle rentals, providing an eco-friendly way to explore the relatively flat city, especially along the scenic riverside area.

River ferries and boat cruises offer a refreshing perspective of the city, particularly enjoyable at sunset when the city lights begin to twinkle along the riverbank.
