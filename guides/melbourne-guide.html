MELBOURNE

Australia's cultural capital blends European sensibilities with Asian influences in a vibrant, art-filled urban landscape.

Melbourne, the capital of Victoria, is widely recognized as Australia's cultural and culinary epicenter. This sophisticated city seamlessly blends Victorian-era architecture with cutting-edge design, creating a dynamic urban landscape that's both quintessentially Australian and distinctly international.

With one of the largest Asian populations in Australia, Melbourne offers a unique fusion of Eastern and Western influences evident in its food scene, festivals, and cultural institutions. The city is characterized by its hidden laneways filled with street art, world-class coffee culture, and creative energy that permeates everything from its restaurants to its thriving arts scene.

TOP ATTRACTIONS

Federation Square

CBD Open daily

Melbourne's central meeting place and cultural precinct housing the Australian Centre for the Moving Image (ACMI), NGV Australia, and various cafes and restaurants.

National Gallery of Victoria (NGV)

Southbank 10:00-17:00

Australia's oldest and most visited art museum with an impressive collection of international and Australian art, including significant Asian exhibitions.

Royal Botanic Gardens

South Yarra 7:30-Sunset

Stunning 38-hectare garden featuring more than 8,500 plant species, including a beautiful Japanese garden and an ornamental lake.

Laneway Street Art

CBD Always accessible

Explore Melbourne's famous graffiti-covered laneways like Hosier Lane, AC/DC Lane, and Centre Place, where street art is celebrated and ever-changing.

Queen Victoria Market

CBD Various hours

Historic market operating since 1878, offering fresh produce, gourmet foods, and unique souvenirs with special night markets during summer.

Chinatown Melbourne

CBD Always open

Australia's oldest Chinatown dating back to the 1850s gold rush, featuring ornate archways, Chinese Museum, and countless authentic restaurants and bubble tea shops.

GETTING AROUND

Local Transportation Tips

Melbourne's tram network is the largest in the world. The Free Tram Zone covers the CBD and makes it easy to explore the city center at no cost.

Purchase a myki card for travel on trams, trains, and buses. It's required for all public transportation outside the Free Tram Zone.

Melbourne is bike-friendly with many dedicated cycling paths. The Melbourne Bike Share program offers convenient bicycle rentals throughout the city.

The CBD is compact and perfect for walking. Many of Melbourne's most interesting areas, like its laneways, are best discovered on foot.
