PHUKET

Thailand's largest island offers a paradise of pristine beaches, azure waters, and vibrant culture amidst lush tropical landscapes.

Phuket, the "Pearl of the Andaman," is Thailand's largest island and a world-renowned tropical paradise. This 49 km long and 21 km wide island features dramatic karst formations, powder-white beaches, and crystal-clear waters on its western coast, while its eastern shores offer a more relaxed atmosphere with mangrove forests and fishing villages.

Beyond its natural splendor, Phuket boasts a rich cultural tapestry shaped by Thai, Chinese, and Portuguese influences. The island's heart, Phuket Town, charms visitors with its colorful Sino-Portuguese architecture, street art, and lively markets. From luxurious beachfront resorts to world-class diving sites, bustling nightlife districts to serene Buddhist temples, Phuket offers a diverse range of experiences that cater to every type of traveler.

TOP ATTRACTIONS

Phang Nga Bay

Northeast of Phuket Day tours available

A breathtaking bay dotted with limestone karsts rising dramatically from emerald-green waters. The area gained fame as "James Bond Island" after appearing in "The Man with the Golden Gun." Explore by traditional longtail boat, kayak through caves, or visit floating Muslim fishing villages.

Old Phuket Town

Central Phuket Best explored early morning or late afternoon

The island's cultural heart, featuring well-preserved Sino-Portuguese architecture from the tin-mining boom of the 19th century. Explore colorful shophouses, hidden cafes, street art, and boutiques along Thalang, Dibuk, and Soi Romanee streets, and visit the Thai Hua Museum to learn about local history.

Big Buddha

Chalong 8:00-19:30 (Daily)

A 45-meter-tall marble Buddha statue perched atop Nakkerd Hill, visible from much of southern Phuket. This spiritual site offers panoramic views of the island, Chalong Bay, and nearby islands. Visitors can sponsor marble slabs used in construction and write messages on them.

Patong Beach

West Coast Open 24 hours

Phuket's most famous beach and nightlife center, offering a 3 km stretch of golden sand. By day, enjoy swimming, jet-skiing, and parasailing; by night, explore Bangla Road's walking street with its bars, clubs, and shows. The area also features the Jungceylon shopping mall and countless restaurants.

Phi Phi Islands

Southeast of Phuket Day tours available

A tropical archipelago made famous by the film "The Beach." Though technically in Krabi province, these stunning islands are a popular day trip from Phuket. Visit Maya Bay, Pileh Lagoon, and Monkey Beach, and enjoy snorkeling in clear turquoise waters teeming with marine life.

Wat Chalong

Chalong 7:00-17:00

Phuket's largest and most important Buddhist temple, dedicated to two revered monks known for their healing abilities. The elaborate complex includes several buildings, a 60m-tall chedi containing a bone fragment of the Buddha, and beautiful pagodas adorned with murals depicting Buddha's life.

WHERE TO EAT

PRU Restaurant

Thalang $$$$

Phuket's first and only Michelin-starred restaurant, located at Trisara resort. Chef Jimmy Ophorst's "farm-to-table" approach showcases sustainable Thai ingredients in an innovative tasting menu. The restaurant's name stands for "Plant, Raise, Understand," reflecting its eco-conscious philosophy.

Ta Khai

Patong $$$

A rustic-luxe seafood restaurant at Rosewood Phuket featuring traditional Thai cuisine in a magical garden setting. Dishes are prepared by a husband-and-wife chef team using century-old recipes and fresh local seafood, creating an authentic dining experience under the stars.

Raya Restaurant

Phuket Town $$

Set in a restored 130-year-old Sino-Portuguese mansion, this local institution serves authentic southern Thai cuisine. Don't miss their signature crab curry with thin rice noodles, caramelized pork belly, and spicy Phuket-style stir-fried noodles—dishes that have attracted Thai royalty.

Bang Pae Seafood

East Coast $$

A hidden gem on Phuket's less-developed east coast, nestled alongside a mangrove forest. This open-air restaurant serves incredibly fresh seafood caught daily, prepared in Thai style. Signature dishes include grilled fish with lemongrass, steamed crab, and tom yum goong.

WHERE TO STAY

Amanpuri

Surin Beach 5-star ultra luxury

Aman's original resort, celebrating 30+ years as Phuket's most exclusive address. Set on a secluded peninsula, this sanctuary features elegant pavilions and villas inspired by traditional Thai architecture, multiple restaurants, a holistic wellness center, a private beach, and exceptional service.

Sri Panwa

Cape Panwa 5-star luxury

A stylish luxury villa resort perched on a peninsula at Phuket's southernmost tip. Each villa features a private infinity pool with breathtaking views. The resort is famous for its Baba Nest rooftop bar—often rated among the world's best—and its Cool Spa with panoramic treatment rooms.

The Slate

Nai Yang Beach 5-star design hotel

A design-focused resort inspired by Phuket's tin mining history, designed by Bill Bensley. The property blends industrial elements with luxurious touches in its uniquely styled suites and pool villas. Located near the airport yet tranquil, it offers eight restaurants, three pools, and a pristine beach.

The Memory at On On Hotel

Phuket Town Boutique heritage hotel

Phuket's oldest hotel (established 1927) and featured in "The Beach" film, this beautifully restored Sino-Portuguese property offers a range of accommodations from budget to boutique rooms. Its central location in Old Town makes it perfect for exploring historical streets and local cuisine.

GETTING AROUND

Local Transportation Tips

Rent a car or scooter for maximum freedom exploring the island. International driver's license required, and drive on the left side. Daily car rental rates start around 1,000 baht, motorbikes from 200 baht. Always check insurance coverage and wear helmets on scooters.

Songthaews (converted pickup trucks) serve as shared taxis with bench seating, following set routes between beaches and Phuket Town. Fares range from 30-50 baht for short trips. Flag one down along main roads, but confirm price before boarding.

Metered taxis are available but drivers often prefer fixed fares. Use the Grab app for fair pricing (typically 300-600 baht between beaches) or negotiate with taxis in advance. Airport taxis have fixed rates posted at the official booth.

Airport buses connect Phuket International Airport to Phuket Town for 100 baht, while hotel-operated and shared minivan services provide affordable connections between popular areas (150-200 baht per person).

Motorcycle taxis (riders with colored vests) offer quick transportation for short distances. Always negotiate the fare before riding (typically 50-100 baht) and request a helmet. Good for navigating traffic in busy areas.

Long-tail boats and speedboats are essential for island-hopping and accessing isolated beaches. Arrange through your hotel or at major beaches. Group tours are economical, while private charters offer flexibility at a premium.
