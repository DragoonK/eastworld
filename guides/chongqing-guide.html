CHONGQING

Experience China's dramatic mountain metropolis, where ancient traditions and futuristic architecture collide in a stunning vertical cityscape.

Chongqing, often called the "Mountain City" and "Fog Capital," is one of China's most distinctive urban landscapes. Built at the confluence of the Yangtze and Jialing rivers, this sprawling metropolis of over 31 million people cascades dramatically across steep karst mountains, creating a three-dimensional cityscape unlike any other in the world.

A former part of Sichuan province that became China's fourth directly-administered municipality in 1997, Chongqing serves as the economic center of the upper Yangtze basin and the gateway to the spectacular Three Gorges region. The city offers visitors a unique blend of experiences: breathtaking urban vistas where skyscrapers seem to sprout directly from mountainsides, ancient villages nestled within the urban sprawl, famously spicy Sichuan cuisine, and a fascinating historical legacy as China's wartime capital during the Second World War.

TOP ATTRACTIONS

Hongyadong (Red Cliff Village)

Yuzhong District 10:00-22:00

This iconic 11-story stilt house complex built into the side of a cliff has become the symbol of Chongqing. The traditional-style building houses restaurants, shops, and teahouses with spectacular views over the Jialing River and city skyline, especially when illuminated at night.

Liziba Monorail Station

Yuzhong District 6:30-22:30

One of Chongqing's most famous urban engineering marvels, where Line 2 of the monorail passes directly through the middle of a residential building between floors 6 and 8. This unique integration of public transportation and residential architecture has become an iconic photo spot.

Three Gorges Museum

Yuzhong District 9:00-17:00 (Closed Mondays)

This extensive museum documents the region's rich history and the massive Three Gorges Dam project. With over 170,000 cultural relics, the exhibits cover Chongqing's role in World War II, Ba-Yu culture, and the impact of the dam on communities and archaeological sites.

Ciqikou Ancient Town

Shapingba District 9:00-21:00

A well-preserved Ming and Qing dynasty port town on the Jialing River that offers a glimpse into old Chongqing. Wander the narrow stone streets lined with traditional wooden buildings, sample local snacks, visit ancient temples, and experience traditional teahouse culture.

Chaotianmen Square

Yuzhong District Open 24 hours

Located at the dramatic confluence of the Yangtze and Jialing rivers, this massive square offers spectacular views of the merging waters and surrounding mountains. It's home to the massive Chongqing Chaotianmen Raffles City complex with its horizontal skyscraper "sky corridor."

Wulong Karst Landscape

Wulong District 8:00-17:00

A UNESCO World Heritage site located about 2 hours from downtown Chongqing, featuring spectacular karst formations including the Three Natural Bridges, Furong Cave, and Houping Tiankeng (sinkhole). The dramatic landscape served as a filming location for "Transformers: Age of Extinction."

WHERE TO EAT

Cygnet Hotpot (Xiaotianshi)

Multiple locations ¥¥

One of Chongqing's most beloved hotpot chains serving authentic local-style hotpot, characterized by its vibrant red broth, numbing Sichuan peppercorns, and intense spiciness. A must-try culinary experience that showcases the city's fiery culinary tradition.

Chen Mapo Doufu

Jiefangbei ¥¥

A branch of the famous Chengdu restaurant that serves the definitive version of mapo tofu, the iconic Sichuan dish of soft tofu in spicy bean sauce with minced pork. The restaurant also serves other Sichuan classics in an upscale setting.

Zhang Liang Fen

Jiefangbei ¥

A local institution serving Chongqing's famous xiaomian (small noodles) with various spicy toppings. This humble noodle shop has been operating for decades and offers an authentic taste of local street food at incredibly affordable prices.

Rafale French Restaurant

InterContinental Chongqing ¥¥¥¥

Upscale dining with spectacular views of the city's skyline and rivers. Located at the top of the InterContinental hotel, this fine dining establishment offers French cuisine with Asian influences and an extensive wine collection in an elegant atmosphere.

GETTING AROUND

Local Transportation Tips

Chongqing's metro system is uniquely adapted to the city's mountainous terrain, with some stations buried deep underground and others elevated high above street level. The system has 9 lines covering major attractions, with fares ranging from ¥2-8.

The Chongqing Rail Transit Line 2 monorail offers some of the city's most spectacular views as it winds through buildings and along the Jialing River. Don't miss the famous section passing through a residential building at Liziba Station.

City buses are inexpensive (¥1-3) but can be challenging to navigate without Chinese language skills. Bus routes 461, 462, and 463 cover major tourist attractions in the central districts. Use the "Chongqing Metro" app for real-time information.

Taxis are abundant and relatively affordable, with starting fares of ¥10 for the first 3km. Note that Chongqing's complex road network and traffic can make taxi journeys time-consuming. Having your destination written in Chinese is essential.

River cruises along the Yangtze and Jialing rivers offer a unique perspective of Chongqing's dramatic cityscape, especially at night when the city is illuminated. Short cruises around the peninsula area cost approximately ¥50-100.

Chongqing's steep topography makes walking challenging but rewarding. The city features numerous pedestrian overpasses, sky bridges, escalators, and elevators connecting different levels of the city, creating a three-dimensional transportation network.
