Why Choose Eastworld Migration?

Eastworld Migration was established in 2001 and has over 20 years of experience helping Asian students successfully migrate to Australia. Located in the heart of Melbourne, we provide comprehensive migration and education services.

Authoritative Service

Comprehensive immigration services including skilled migration, work visas, employer sponsorship, business migration, spouse visas, and student visas with accompanying family members.

Caring Service

Representing various Australian institutions, providing free course selection guidance, free application and processing for all types of transfers and enrollments.

Specialized Asian Focus

Specialized expertise in helping Asian students with cultural understanding, language support, and tailored guidance for smooth transition to Australian life.

Expert Team

All migration advisors hold Australian government-regulated Migration Agent Registration Authority (MARA) certificates. Education advisors hold QEAC certificates.

Migration Services

Our experienced migration experts stay up-to-date with all visa and immigration policies, creating customized migration solutions for each client's individual circumstances.

  • Skilled migration (189/190/491)
  • Employer sponsorship (482/186)
  • Business and investment visas
  • Partner and family visas
  • AAT appeal representation

Settlement Support

Beyond visas and applications, we provide comprehensive settlement support to ensure your successful integration into Australian society.

  • Accommodation assistance

  • Community integration support
